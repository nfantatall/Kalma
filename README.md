# Kalma

**Listening, orientation, and community for mental health.**

## The challenge
About 100 million people in Africa live with mental disorders (WHO), yet 85% receive no treatment, with only about 0.1 psychiatrist per 100,000 people. Stigma and the concentration of professionals in big cities keep help out of reach. Most digital tools require a smartphone, data, and literacy.

## Our solution
Kalma is a multichannel platform offering anonymous, fully confidential mental health listening and orientation:
- **SMS listening**: the user sends a text, the system silently analyzes it and connects them to appropriate help.
- **Voice AI by phone call**: no app required. The AI understands and responds in the local language, and transfers immediately to a human if a distress signal is detected.
- **Multilingual mobile app**: connects users to professionals and support groups.

## Business model
- **B2C**: freemium subscription (about $1/month after one free month) and commission on consultations with professionals.
- **B2G/B2B**: institutional funding (health insurance, Ministry of Health, youth employment programs) and subscriptions from universities and employers.

## Status
Early stage: the solution and user journeys are designed. We are preparing a pilot.
